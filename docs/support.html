Frequently asked questions

How do I add points?

Tap +1, +2, or +3 under a team's name to add that many points to its score.

I tapped the wrong button. How do I fix the score?

Tap the pencil button next to the +1/+2/+3 buttons, enter the correct score, and tap Save.

How do I start a new game?

Tap the menu button (☰) in the top-left corner and choose New Score. Pick the team names and colors, then tap Start. The finished game is saved to History.

How do I change team names or colors?

Open the menu (☰) and choose Settings. Edit the name and color for each team, then tap Save.

Where can I see past games?

Open the menu (☰) and choose History. Swipe a game to the left to delete it, or tap Clear All to remove everything.

Is my data backed up or shared?

No. Everything stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app also deletes your scores and history. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

BBall Score Keeper runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, in portrait and landscape.

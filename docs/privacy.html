Privacy Policy

BBall Score Keeper · Last updated: 25 November 2026
We don't collect, track, or share any of your data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Scores, team names, game history, and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it, and we can't see them. Deleting the app removes this data permanently.

Sharing of information

We don't share any information with third parties, because we don't collect any.

Tracking, analytics, and ads

The app contains no tracking, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete games in the app or delete the app itself. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
